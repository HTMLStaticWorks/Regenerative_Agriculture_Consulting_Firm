About TerraSoil Consulting Firm

Learn about our mission, our team of certified agronomists and soil microbiologists, and our science-based commitment to soil health.

Founded in 2011

Advancing Ecological Agriculture & Soil Microbiome Health

TerraSoil was established with a clear goal: to bridge the gap between cutting-edge soil microbiology and practical farm management. Our founders saw how heavy reliance on synthetic fertilizers depleted natural soil organic matter (SOM), leaving crops vulnerable to drought and disease.

Scientific Precision

All recommendations rely on rigorous ISO-certified lab bio-scans and field data.

Ecological

We build long-term biological fertility that protects soil, water, and climate resilience.

Farmer Profitability

We optimize synthetic inputs to lower production costs while improving crop yields.

Our Scientific Methodology

A Tailored Science Roadmap for Every Farm Operation

We recognize that every soil type, climate, and crop rotation requires a custom approach. Compaction layers, low microbial activity, and poor water infiltration cannot be solved with generic off-the-shelf fertilizers.

Our consultants integrate PLFA biological testing, Haney soil health scores, and targeted cover crop mixes to rebuild soil carbon and create resilient farm ecosystems.
