Land Health Risks

Common Threats to Commercial Yield & Soil Health

Continuous synthetic inputs and tillage erode soil structure over time, leaving farm operations exposed to rising costs and weather extremes.

Soil Compaction & Hardpan

Heavy machinery and subsoil hardpans restrict root growth, stopping deep moisture access during dry spells.

Nutrient Leaching & Runoff

Soluble nitrogen washes out into groundwater, leading to lost input dollars and environmental compliance issues.

Drought Vulnerability

Low soil organic matter reduces field water storage, causing heat stress and yield dips during rain shortages.

Rising Input Expenditure

Escalating costs for synthetic fertilizers and chemical treatments compress farm operating margins every season.
