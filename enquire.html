Quick Enquiry FAQs

Is there any fee to submit an enquiry?

No, submitting a proposal enquiry and your initial discovery call with our agronomist team are completely complimentary.

Do I need past soil test results?

Not at all. If you have past Haney or PLFA reports, you can share them, but TerraSoil offers full GPS soil sampling as part of our consultation packages.

Are your plans NRCS EQIP compliant?

Yes, our certified crop advisors (CCA) design cover crop mixes and nutrient management plans that meet USDA-NRCS technical standards.
