Farm Ecosystem Transformations

Side-by-side comparative analysis of degraded conventional fields vs. biological soil systems under TerraSoil management.

Case Study 1: 2,400-Acre Midwest Grain Farm

Conventional Tilled State

Hardpan compaction at 8-inch depth, heavy water runoff during rain, and $220/acre chemical input costs.

Regenerated Humus Soil

3.8% SOM (+1.4% gain), 4-inch/hr infiltration rate, and 35% reduction in synthetic nitrogen needs.

Case Study 2: Texas Pasture & Grazing Ranch

Continuous Grazing Pasture

Weed invasion, low forage regrowth rate, bare soil patches, and poor cattle weight gain during summer.

AMP Rotational System

Doubled grazing days per acre, lush warm-season grass diversity, and rapid animal weight gain.
